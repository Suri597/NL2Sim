Our supply chain starts with a foam supplier called FoamTech, which ships raw foam to a manufacturing plant. There's also a separate hardware supplier, BoltWorks, that supplies metal fasteners to the same plant. The plant, called Cushion Factory, assembles foam and fasteners into finished office chairs. Finished chairs are shipped to a regional distribution center before reaching two customers: OfficeMart and a smaller buyer called DeskHub. There is also a returns processing facility that handles defective chairs sent back by OfficeMart.
