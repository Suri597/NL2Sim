I run a small clothing company making two products: t-shirts and hoodies. Both are made from cotton fabric, but hoodies use about three times as much fabric as t-shirts. My factory can produce about 50 t-shirts a day or 15 hoodies a day, depending on what it's running. Finished shirts and hoodies get stored in two regional warehouses — one on the East Coast, one on the West Coast — before shipping out. I have two retail customers: one on the East Coast that orders about 30 t-shirts and 10 hoodies a day, and one on the West Coast that orders about 15 t-shirts and 5 hoodies a day. Simulate 90 days.
