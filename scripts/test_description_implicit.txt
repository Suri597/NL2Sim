Here's what you need to know to run our production floor.

Two starting inputs come from outside: a base compound supplied by Baseline Chem, and a solvent supplied by Pure Solvents. Base compound arrives in batches of 25,000-35,000 units with a constant 3-day lead time at 15 per unit. Solvent arrives in batches of 15,000-20,000 units with a constant 2-day lead time at 22 per unit. Both start at zero stock.

The reaction step happens at ReactorCore, combining the base compound and solvent into what we just call "intermediate X" internally. That reaction takes a constant 2.5 days per cycle. Zero starting stock on all three materials there.

From intermediate X, two separate finishing lines take over. LineA finishes it into ProductAlpha, a 2-day process. LineB finishes the same intermediate into ProductBeta instead, a 3-day process. Both lines start at zero for everything.

ProductAlpha gets staged at AlphaStock before shipping, currently holding 40,000 units. ProductBeta gets staged separately at BetaStock, currently holding 35,000 units.

On the customer side: SingleBuyer only ever wants ProductAlpha, ordering every 6 days in batches of 5,000-9,000 units, 2-day delivery, priced at 60 per unit, paid back in 18 days. BetaOnly only wants ProductBeta, ordering every 9 days, 6,000-10,000 units, 3-day delivery, 75 per unit, paid back in 22 days. DualBuyer is different -- they take both product lines: ProductAlpha on a 10-day cycle in 3,000-6,000 unit batches, and separately ProductBeta on a 12-day cycle in 4,000-7,000 unit batches, both with 4-day delivery, ProductAlpha priced at 60 and ProductBeta at 75, payment terms 20 days for both.

Lost sales apply across the board if we're short.

For the sim: 200-day horizon, daily steps, no warm-up, 10 replications, 95% confidence intervals on inventory and cash please.
