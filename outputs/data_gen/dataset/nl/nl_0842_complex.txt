This network makes tablet devices using resin and gold_wire as the incoming materials, with all activity centered on the Production Center and finished stock moving on to the Fulfillment Hub. Along the way, the plant also builds 2 supporting sub-assemblies: each cooling_unit consumes 5 units of resin, while each heat_sink uses 4 units of gold_wire and 1 unit of resin. The finished tablet itself is fairly material-intensive, requiring 4 units of resin, 3 units of cooling_unit, 2 units of heat_sink, and 2 units of gold_wire for every unit produced.
