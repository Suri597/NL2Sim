This network is built around producing routers, with raw materials flowing from MetalFab Inc., MegaSupply, and FastChip Corp. into Main Factory, and finished routers then moving on to the Logistics Center. Each router is assembled from 3 units of epoxy_compound, 4 units of copper_wire, and 4 units of carbon_fiber. At Main Factory, the bonding step converts those inputs into the finished router, and that production cycle is fixed at 10.0 weeks. The site manages inventory for router, copper_wire, carbon_fiber, and epoxy_compound, while the operation is supported by a Laser Cutter with capacity for 4 units, a constant service time of 0.245 weeks, and an operating cost of $10.763 per week. Copper_wire is sourced from MetalFab Inc. under a min-max style replenishment approach: when stock falls below 905.82 units, it is brought back up to 2950.48 units. MetalFab charges $230.84 per unit, typically delivers on an exponential schedule averaging 12.0 weeks, offers 45.0-day payment terms, and can supply up to 959524 units per order. Carbon_fiber comes from MegaSupply and is ordered as needed based on demand. Its lead time is exactly 21.0 weeks, the purchase cost is $22.322 per unit, payment falls somewhere between 52.0 days and 77.0 days after receipt, and supplier capacity is capped at 459730 units. Epoxy_compound is purchased from FastChip Corp. using another threshold policy, with replenishment triggered below 2305.48 units and restoring stock to 5102.53 units. FastChip charges $327.005 per unit, lead time varies uniformly between 15.0 weeks and 21.0 weeks, and invoices are paid 43.0 days after delivery. On the inventory side, copper_wire carries a shortage cost of $5.838 per unit and is reviewed every 16 weeks, while epoxy_compound has a holding cost of $7.549 per unit and a 22-week review cycle. Finished router stock starts at 825550 units, with a holding cost of $1.005 per unit and daily review effectively every 1 week. Customer demand comes from HP Enterprise, which places router orders every 35.0 weeks. Order size ranges from 2560778.0 units to 3192548.0 units, delivery to the customer takes between 11.0 weeks and 20.0 weeks, and routers sell at $460.681 per unit. If demand exceeds available inventory, the business ships what it can and loses the remainder. Customer payments are received between 20.0 days and 82.0 days later. The model runs for 500 weeks in total, with results collected after a 30-week warm-up period, and it is evaluated across 5 replications.
