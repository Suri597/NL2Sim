This setup centers on making laptops, with Main Factory handling production and Fulfillment Hub serving as the downstream warehouse, while FastChip Corp., Memory Star, and PolymerSource feed the operation with raw materials. Each laptop is built from 4 units of aluminum_sheet and 3 units of gold_wire. Gold wire is managed with a min-max style replenishment rule: when stock drops below 1872.88 units, it is brought back up to 4172.97 units, and that item is reviewed every 1 day; shortages on gold_wire are valued at $41.226 per unit. Aluminum sheet follows a similar approach, with replenishment triggered below 2575.37 units and raised to 8656.78 units, reviewed every 15 days, and carrying a shortage cost of $32.501 per unit. Supply for gold_wire comes from two sources: FastChip Corp. delivers in anywhere from 12.0 days to 24.0 days, charges $170.784 per unit, offers 42.0-day payment terms, and can provide up to 805438 units per order; PolymerSource also supplies gold_wire, with lead times averaging 23.0 days, a unit cost of $279.128, payment due between 46.0 days and 62.0 days after delivery, and capacity up to 966713 units, with an added 6.0-day transfer to Main Factory. Aluminum_sheet is purchased from Memory Star at $285.329 per unit, with lead time averaging 15.0 days and payment due between 53.0 days and 59.0 days. At Main Factory, the assembly step converts aluminum_sheet and gold_wire into laptops, and each production cycle varies uniformly between 15.0 days and 25.0 days. Finished laptops start with 1506229 units in stock, are reviewed every 1 day, and incur a holding cost of $4.846 per unit. Demand comes from Siemens Digital, which places orders every 44.0 days for exactly 2412703.0 units, expects delivery in 5.0 days to 10.0 days, and pays 47.0 days after receipt; the selling price is $1578.221 per unit. If laptops are unavailable, the sale is simply lost. The modeled network links all 3 suppliers to the 2 facilities, with laptop flow moving from Main Factory to Fulfillment Hub, and the scenario is evaluated over a 100-day horizon with 5 replications.
