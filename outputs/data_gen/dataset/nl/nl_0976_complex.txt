This network revolves around a single manufacturing site, Pilot Line, supported by a Distribution Center and supplied by GlobalComponents, PolymerSource, CarbonSupply Co., SiliconWorks, Memory Star, GlassCore Ltd., and NanoMaterials Inc. The raw inputs are aluminum_sheet, lithium_cell, and carbon_fiber, and those materials are turned into several in-house subassemblies before final products are shipped out as drone, industrial_sensor, and CPU. The product recipes are fairly material-intensive: each lens_assembly uses 2 units of aluminum_sheet and 3 units of carbon_fiber, each motor_assembly consumes 3 units of lithium_cell, 3 units of aluminum_sheet, and 2 units of carbon_fiber, and each sensor_array requires 2 units of carbon_fiber and 5 units of lithium_cell. At the finished-goods level, a drone needs 2 units of lithium_cell, 2 units of aluminum_sheet, 2 units of motor_assembly, and 3 units of carbon_fiber; an industrial_sensor uses 2 units of carbon_fiber and 4 units of motor_assembly; and a CPU is built from 4 units of sensor_array, 2 units of lens_assembly, and 4 units of lithium_cell.
