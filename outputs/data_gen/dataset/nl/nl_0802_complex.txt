This network revolves around Main Factory, supported by a Fulfillment Hub, and it makes three finished goods: tablet, GPU, and camera. The plant also builds a key sub-assembly called lens_assembly, which uses 3 units of resin, 4 units of silicon_wafer, 4 units of steel_rod, and 1 unit of ceramic_substrate. That sub-assembly is then consumed downstream: each tablet needs 2 units of silicon_wafer plus 4 units of lens_assembly, each GPU requires 2 units of ceramic_substrate, 3 units of steel_rod, 1 unit of resin, and 4 units of lens_assembly, and each camera uses 3 units of resin with 4 units of steel_rod. On the inventory side, silicon_wafer is sourced as needed, with a holding cost of $5.708 per unit and a 29-week review cadence. Ceramic_substrate is also replenished on demand. Resin follows the same as-needed approach, with holding running at $0.672 per unit and review every 17 weeks. Steel_rod is handled more tightly: when stock drops below 3491.34 units, it is replenished up to 11454.24 units, with a holding cost of $1.878 per unit, a shortage penalty of $40.335 per unit, and review every 28 weeks. Starting stock is very large for several items, including 3687908 units of lens_assembly, 3513200 units of tablet, 4503834 units of GPU, and 163083 units of camera. GPU shortages carry a cost of $24.78 per unit with review every 1 week, while camera shortages are valued at $31.431 per unit and are also checked every 1 week. Supply into Main Factory comes from a broad vendor base. MegaSupply provides silicon_wafer with a lead time between 10.0 weeks and 11.0 weeks, charging $405.078 per unit, and payment is due between 9.0 weeks and 43.0 weeks after receipt. GlobalComponents is the alternate silicon_wafer source, delivering in exactly 19.0 weeks, at $319.513 per unit, with payment terms ranging from 19.0 weeks to 29.0 weeks; it can ship up to 339444 units. Ceramic_substrate comes from Memory Star in a fixed 8.0 weeks at $416.586 per unit with 18.0-week payment terms, or from GlassCore Ltd. with an exponential lead time averaging 6.0 weeks, a lower cost of $203.906 per unit, payment between 24.0 weeks and 49.0 weeks, and capacity of 906907 units. Resin is available from BatteryTech with lead times between 17.0 weeks and 22.0 weeks, a unit cost of $381.472, net 31.0 weeks to pay, and a maximum order size of 97394 units; shipments from BatteryTech to Main Factory take an additional 2.0 weeks to transfer. CarbonSupply Co. is the cheaper resin option at $52.356 per unit, delivered in exactly 8.0 weeks with 14.0-week payment terms and capacity of 478228 units, and its transfer into Main Factory takes 3.0 weeks. Steel_rod is sourced from ElectroParts with lead times ranging from 4.0 weeks to 10.0 weeks, cost of $295.804 per unit, and payment between 27.0 weeks and 55.0 weeks, or from SiliconWorks with an exponential lead time averaging 26.0 weeks, cost of $466.409 per unit, and payment due between 33.0 weeks and 37.0 weeks. Production is concentrated at Main Factory. The cutting step turns silicon_wafer and lens_assembly into tablet in a fixed 2.0 weeks and requires the Quality Scanner. That scanner has capacity for 1 unit at a time, works with service times between 0.948 weeks and 1.875 weeks, and runs in batches of 13 units with a maximum wait of 12.696 weeks. It is not perfectly reliable either: uptime is a steady 131.491 weeks, downtime is exponential with an average of 28.353 weeks, and operating cost is $12.081 per week. A second resource, the Assembly Line, has capacity of 5 units, service time between 2.625 weeks and 7.111 weeks, and operating cost of $11.486 per week. GPU production at Main Factory is handled through the testing step, which consumes ceramic_substrate, steel_rod, resin, and lens_assembly and runs for exactly 20.0 weeks. Camera production uses the welding step, taking resin and steel_rod and finishing in anywhere from 5.0 weeks to 22.0 weeks. Finished goods then move from Main Factory to the Fulfillment Hub, specifically tablet, GPU, and camera. On the demand side, Dell Technologies buys tablet, placing orders every 21.0 weeks to 56.0 weeks, each order for 1552271.0 units, with customer delivery in 6.0 weeks. Tablets sell at $18.425 per unit, payment arrives between 44.0 weeks and 82.0 weeks, and if stock is unavailable the sale is lost. NXP Semiconductors orders GPU with interarrival averaging 47.0 weeks, demand anywhere from 112789.0 units to 4230566.0 units, and a consistent 12.0-week customer lead time. GPUs sell at $1671.66 per unit, payment is received 11.0 weeks later, and the policy is partial fulfillment—available units ship, and the remainder is lost. Schneider Electric purchases camera every 43.0 weeks, with demand centered around 3446894.0 units and varying by about 722235.0 units, and delivery takes 7.0 weeks. Cameras are priced at $2034.928 per unit, paid 20.0 weeks after shipment, and any shortfall is handled the same way: ship what is on hand and lose the rest. The modeled network includes all 8 suppliers feeding Main Factory, with Main Factory and the Fulfillment Hub as the 2 operating sites, and the study runs over a 500-week horizon with 10 replications.
