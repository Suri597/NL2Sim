This setup revolves around two finished goods, GPU and router, built from ceramic_substrate and solder_paste and flowing through Process Go., MetalFab Inc., Main Factory, and finally the Warehouse. On the inbound side, ceramic_substrate is managed with a min-max style replenishment rule: when stock drops below 3627.92 units, it is brought back up to 4725.67 units. Process Go. supplies that material at $336.52 per unit, with a lead time fixed at 6.0 days, and payment is due 53.0 days after delivery. Once shipped to Main Factory, the ceramic_substrate takes between 1.0 and 4.0 days to transfer. Solder_paste is also controlled by an inventory threshold approach, reordered when it falls below 3799.57 units and topped up to 8887.96 units. MetalFab Inc. provides solder_paste at $248.143 per unit, with supply timing following an exponential pattern averaging 3.0 days, and payment terms ranging from 46.0 days to 83.0 days. Solder_paste also carries a shortage cost of $4.628 per unit and is reviewed every 12 days. At Main Factory, one manufacturing flow is polishing, where solder_paste is turned into GPU in a cycle that ranges from 6.0 to 10.0 days. Each GPU consumes 4 units of solder_paste. A second flow at the same Main Factory is fabrication, where 1 unit of ceramic_substrate and 4 units of solder_paste are used to make each router, and that process takes between 9.0 and 17.0 days. Finished GPUs start with 1407197 units in stock, incur a holding cost of $8.95 per unit, and are reviewed every 1 day. Routers begin with 478550 units on hand, have a shortage cost of $16.919 per unit, and are also reviewed every 1 day. After production, both GPU and router move from Main Factory to Warehouse. GPU orders are delivered in 28.0 days, any unfilled demand is backordered, the selling price is $3607.629 per unit, and payment comes 10.0 days after delivery. Bosch Automotive buys router on a slower rhythm, with orders arriving every 47.0 days and demand averaging 1015955.0 units with variability of 281855.0 units. Router shipments reach the customer in 14.0 days, available stock is shipped and the rest of the demand is lost, the sales price is $211.691 per unit, and customer payment lands between 43.0 and 62.0 days later. The network links Process Go. and MetalFab Inc. to Main Factory, and Main Factory to Warehouse, and the whole scenario is evaluated over a 500-day horizon with 10 replications.
