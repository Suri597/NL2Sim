This network revolves around two finished electronics lines, FPGA and SoC, supported by raw inputs from MetalFab Inc., NanoMaterials Inc., and Memory Star, with production centered at the Integration Center and storage flowing through the Logistics Center. The basic inputs are memory_chip and plastic_pellet. A battery_pack is built as an internal sub-assembly using 5 units of memory_chip and 3 units of plastic_pellet, while FPGA consumes 1 unit of plastic_pellet plus 2 units of memory_chip per unit, and SoC is made from 4 units of battery_pack per unit. Memory_chip is sourced on an as-needed basis, with inventory reviewed every 8 days and a holding cost of $0.91 per unit. MetalFab Inc. supplies memory_chip with an exponential lead time averaging 21 days, at a cost of $162.258 per unit, and payment falls due between 27 days and 48 days after receipt. Memory Star is the second memory_chip source, with an exponential lead time averaging 5 days, a unit cost of $289.428, payment terms ranging from 42 days to 76 days, and a maximum order size of 277389 units; shipments from Memory Star to the Integration Center also take between 7 days and 9 days to transfer. Plastic_pellet, meanwhile, arrives in scheduled deliveries every 18 days, with lot sizes varying uniformly between 33102 units and 82936 units. That material comes from NanoMaterials Inc., whose lead time averages 1 day, charges $357.234 per unit, offers net 77 days payment, and can supply up to 258048 units per delivery. Plastic_pellet inventory is reviewed every 13 days, and any shortage carries a penalty of $18.563 per unit. On the production side, the Integration Center handles both manufacturing steps. One assembly flow converts plastic_pellet and memory_chip into FPGA, with each run taking between 11 days and 13 days. A separate molding flow turns battery_pack into SoC in exactly 29 days. Starting stock is quite large: 1569097 units of battery_pack, 4185287 units of FPGA, and 2046641 units of SoC. FPGA inventory is checked every 1 day, with holding cost at $3.172 per unit and shortage cost at $33.297 per unit. SoC is also reviewed every 1 day, and shortages cost $45.694 per unit. Finished goods move from the Integration Center to the Logistics Center for distribution.
