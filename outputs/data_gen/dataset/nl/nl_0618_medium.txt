This network revolves around three incoming materials—solder_paste, memory_chip, and microprocessor—and two saleable products, SoC and solar_panel, moving through Assembly Hub and then on to the Regional Depot over a 365-day planning window, with 10 replications in the study. At the product level, each SoC consumes 2 units of solder_paste and 2 units of microprocessor, while each solar_panel uses 1 unit of memory_chip. Solder_paste is sourced on an as-needed basis from GlassCore Ltd., with a supplier lead time fixed at 20 days, a purchase cost of $203.831 per unit, 84-day payment terms, and a maximum supply of 463446 units per order; its shortage cost is $10.377 per unit and it is reviewed every 28 days. Memory_chip is also replenished based on demand, coming from PolymerSource in exactly 20 days at $246.003 per unit, with payment due 80 days after receipt and supplier capacity capped at 233612 units; the shipment from PolymerSource to Assembly Hub takes 13 days. Microprocessor supply works differently: it arrives in periodic lots ranging from 22923.0 units to 74006.0 units, with scheduled deliveries every 9 days. ChipCo provides this item with an exponential lead time averaging 10 days, at a unit cost of $440.834, and invoices are paid 76 days after delivery; the transfer from ChipCo to Assembly Hub varies between 4 and 7 days. Microprocessor inventory carries a shortage penalty of $44.87 per unit and is reviewed every 2 days. Manufacturing is concentrated at Assembly Hub, which handles solder_paste, microprocessor, SoC, solar_panel, and memory_chip. One fabrication step turns solder_paste and microprocessor into SoC, with a processing time anywhere from 9 to 26 days. A separate coating step converts memory_chip into solar_panel in a consistent 3 days. Finished goods then move from Assembly Hub to the Regional Depot for stocking and fulfillment. Starting inventory is substantial: 3184819 units of SoC and 2743906 units of solar_panel. SoC inventory is reviewed every 1 day and faces a shortage cost of $19.232 per unit. Solar_panel stock is also checked every 1 day, with a holding cost of $5.546 per unit and a shortage cost of $44.742 per unit. On the demand side, Broadcom Limited buys SoC, placing orders with exponentially distributed interarrival times averaging 42 days. Order size is typically 3157652.0 units with variability of about 192914.0 units, delivery to the customer takes 19 days, and the selling price is $146.464 per unit. If inventory is short, available units ship immediately and the remainder is backordered, and Broadcom pays 56 days after delivery. TechCorp purchases solar_panel with customer arrivals averaging 59 days, and each order is a constant 2669767.0 units. Delivery lead time to TechCorp ranges from 8 to 17 days, the sales price is $2815.693 per unit, and payment comes in between 32 and 34 days. Their orders follow the same partial-fulfillment backorder approach, so whatever is on hand ships first and the balance waits for replenishment.
