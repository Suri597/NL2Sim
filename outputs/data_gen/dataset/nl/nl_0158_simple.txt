The resin is sourced on an as-needed basis, so purchasing is triggered by demand rather than a fixed replenishment calendar. At the raw material level, resin carries a holding cost of $6.738 per unit and a shortage cost of $40.099 per unit, with inventory reviewed every 14 days. Production at Plant A happens through a molding step, where 2 units of resin are consumed to produce 1 unit of CPU, and each production cycle takes a consistent 27 days. On the finished goods side, CPU inventory starts at 4319063 units, with a holding cost of $4.535 per unit and review taking place every 1 day. After production, CPUs are transferred from Plant A to the Warehouse, which acts as the stocking point for customer fulfillment. ABB Industrial places orders for CPU every 11 days, each order being a fixed 700408 units, and expects delivery in exactly 21 days. If demand exceeds available stock, available units are shipped immediately and the remainder is backordered. Each CPU sells for $1636.238 per unit, and customer payments come in somewhere between 18 days and 36 days. The network is straightforward: Process Go. feeds Plant A with resin, and Plant A sends CPU output to the Warehouse. The model runs over 730 days, with results measured after a 30-day warm-up period, and includes 10 replications.
