This network makes GPUs, drones, and laptops using resin, ceramic_substrate, and solder_paste as the basic inputs, with Memory Star, SiliconWorks, and MegaSupply feeding a Manufacturing Site and finished goods then moving on to a Storage Facility. The bill of materials is fairly layered: each sensor_array uses 3 units of resin, each cooling_unit requires 2 units of solder_paste, 4 units of resin, and 4 units of ceramic_substrate, and each circuit_board consumes 1 unit of ceramic_substrate, 4 units of solder_paste, and 5 units of resin. On the finished side, a GPU is built from 3 units of solder_paste, 1 unit of circuit_board, 2 units of resin, and 2 units of cooling_unit; a drone needs 4 units of sensor_array, 2 units of cooling_unit, and 4 units of solder_paste; and a laptop uses 4 units of resin and 4 units of ceramic_substrate.
