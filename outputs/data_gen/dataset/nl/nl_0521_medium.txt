This network centers on two finished goods, industrial_sensor and laptop, built from gold_wire and steel_rod and flowing through Manufacturing Site before shipment to the Logistics Center. Gold_wire is sourced from GlassCore Ltd. on a demand-triggered basis, with supply arriving in exactly 10.0 weeks; each unit costs $426.446, and payment to that supplier falls somewhere between 44.0 weeks and 77.0 weeks after receipt. Steel_rod is also replenished only as needed, coming from GlobalComponents in a fixed 6.0 weeks at $330.047 per unit, with net 10.0 weeks payment terms and a maximum order size of 531896 units. On the raw material side, gold_wire carries a shortage cost of $34.828 per unit and is reviewed every 17 weeks, while steel_rod has a shortage penalty of $2.191 per unit with the same 17-week review rhythm. Production happens at Manufacturing Site in 2 streams: a coating step converts 1 unit of steel_rod plus 1 unit of gold_wire into 1 unit of industrial_sensor, and that cycle varies uniformly between 3.0 weeks and 4.0 weeks; separately, an assembly step turns 4 units of gold_wire into 1 unit of laptop, with processing time ranging from 3.0 weeks to 18.0 weeks. The site manages inventory for industrial_sensor, gold_wire, steel_rod, and laptop, and finished goods then move from Manufacturing Site to the Logistics Center. Starting stock is very large, at 3965842 units of industrial_sensor and 2562624 units of laptop. Industrial_sensor inventory incurs a holding cost of $2.095 per unit, a shortage cost of $19.871 per unit, and is reviewed every 1 week; laptop inventory is reviewed every 1 week as well and has a shortage cost of $25.837 per unit. On the demand side, Infineon Technologies buys industrial_sensor, placing orders somewhere between every 22.0 weeks and 45.0 weeks, with demand centered around 4459730.0 units and varying by about 125514.0 units. Delivery to that customer takes between 5.0 weeks and 18.0 weeks, the selling price is $1528.128 per unit, payment arrives 12.0 weeks after delivery, and under the partial shipment policy whatever is available is sent while the remaining demand is lost. Broadcom Limited purchases laptop, with orders arriving exponentially at an average of 23.0 weeks, each for a constant 1794157.0 units. Customer lead time for laptop runs from 5.0 weeks to 15.0 weeks, the price is $1084.929 per unit, and payment is received between 31.0 weeks and 54.0 weeks; here too, available units are shipped and any shortfall is lost. The modeled network explicitly links GlassCore Ltd. and GlobalComponents to Manufacturing Site, and then sends both industrial_sensor and laptop onward to the Logistics Center. The scenario is evaluated over a 500-week horizon with 10 replications.
