This network revolves around three incoming materials—copper_wire, epoxy_compound, and steel_rod—feeding an Assembly Hub that makes two sellable items, tablet and FPGA, with a Fulfillment Hub handling outbound flow. One in-house built sub-assembly, sensor_array, is also part of the mix: each sensor_array uses 4 units of steel_rod, 4 units of copper_wire, and 1 unit of epoxy_compound. The tablet itself is made from 1 unit of epoxy_compound and 4 units of copper_wire, while each FPGA requires 4 units of steel_rod plus 3 units of sensor_array.
