This network is built around making laptops, with PolymerSource, GlobalComponents, and MegaSupply feeding a Processing Unit, and finished laptops then moving on to the Distribution Center. Each laptop requires 3 units of carbon_fiber, 4 units of gold_wire, and 4 units of lithium_cell. At the plant, the cutting step converts carbon_fiber, gold_wire, and lithium_cell into laptops, and that work takes anywhere from 2 weeks to 14 weeks per cycle. The Processing Unit manages lithium_cell, laptop, gold_wire, and carbon_fiber inventory directly, while the Distribution Center serves as the warehouse downstream of production. Gold_wire is sourced on demand from PolymerSource, with supply arriving in between 14 weeks and 26 weeks, at a cost of $430.516 per unit, under 87 days payment terms, and the supplier can deliver up to 948545 units per order. Gold_wire inventory is reviewed every 26 weeks, and shortages carry a penalty of $33.446 per unit. Carbon_fiber is also ordered as needed, coming from GlobalComponents with a lead time ranging from 14 weeks to 26 weeks, a purchase cost of $366.904 per unit, and payment due 30 days after receipt. Its stock is checked every 5 weeks, with a holding cost of $4.955 per unit. Lithium_cell supply comes from MegaSupply on a fixed 18-week lead time at $489.278 per unit, with net 21 days payment. This item uses a min-max approach: when inventory falls below 1669.21 units, an order is triggered to raise it back to 5773.3 units. Lithium_cell stock is reviewed every 25 weeks, and any shortfall costs $5.864 per unit. On the finished goods side, laptop inventory starts at 846420 units, is reviewed every 1 week, and has a shortage cost of $37.571 per unit. Customer demand comes from ABB Industrial, with orders arriving somewhere between 22 weeks and 36 weeks apart, and each order requesting between 2582491 units and 3508507 units. Customer shipments are promised in exactly 20 weeks, and the business uses a partial backorder approach, meaning available units ship immediately while the remainder waits for replenishment. Laptops are sold at $2805.439 per unit, and ABB Industrial pays 31 days after delivery. The modeled flow is straightforward: gold_wire, carbon_fiber, and lithium_cell move from their respective suppliers into the Processing Unit, laptops are produced there, and the finished product is transferred to the Distribution Center. The scenario is evaluated over a 500-week horizon with 5 replications.
