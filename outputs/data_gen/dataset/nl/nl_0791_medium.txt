This network makes industrial_sensor units using 2 incoming materials, gold_wire and lithium_cell, with ChipCo, MegaSupply, and MetalFab Inc. feeding a Processing Unit and finished goods then moving on to a Regional Depot. Each industrial_sensor requires 3 units of gold_wire and 2 units of lithium_cell. Gold_wire is sourced on an as-needed basis, while lithium_cell follows a min-max style replenishment rule that triggers when stock drops below 3341.98 units and lifts inventory back up to 6425.69 units. The gold_wire stock carries a holding cost of $6.58 per unit, a shortage penalty of $32.792 per unit, and is reviewed every 21 days; lithium_cell is reviewed every 18 days with a holding cost of $0.662 per unit and a shortage cost of $26.216 per unit. On the supply side, ChipCo provides gold_wire with an exponential lead time averaging 17.0 days at a cost of $282.414 per unit, and payment is due exactly 12.0 days after delivery. MetalFab Inc. is the alternate gold_wire source, much faster with an exponential lead time averaging 1.0 days, but at a higher $430.124 per unit, with payment due 77.0 days after receipt. MegaSupply sends lithium_cell with an exponential lead time averaging 11.0 days, charges $237.218 per unit, and offers payment terms somewhere between 48.0 days and 81.0 days. Manufacturing happens at the Processing Unit through an assembly step that takes between 13.0 days and 15.0 days per cycle, converting gold_wire and lithium_cell into industrial_sensor. The operation is supported by a Robot Arm with capacity for 3 units, an exponential service time with a mean of 6.894 days, and an operating cost of $6.309 per day. Finished industrial_sensor inventory starts at 2477941 units, is reviewed every 1 day, and incurs a shortage cost of $39.305 per unit. Demand comes from Broadcom Limited, with customer orders arriving exponentially with a mean of 46.0 days, and each order is a fixed 4498024.0 units. Customers wait if stock is unavailable, so demand is backordered rather than lost. Delivery to Broadcom Limited takes between 12.0 days and 23.0 days, the product sells at $2152.384 per unit, and customer payment is received exactly 57.0 days later. The model runs for 200 days, with the first 50 days treated as warm-up, and results are based on 10 replications.
