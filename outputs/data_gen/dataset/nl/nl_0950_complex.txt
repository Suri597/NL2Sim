This network revolves around a Fab site that sources microprocessor and steel_rod inputs from Memory Star, GlobalComponents, MetalFab Inc., and SiliconWorks, then pushes finished SoC, tablet, and smartphone volumes on to a Regional Depot. There is also an internal build stage for circuit_board, which acts like a fabricated component: each circuit_board consumes 2 units of steel_rod and 1 unit of microprocessor. The end items have different build recipes as well. One SoC requires 3 units of steel_rod and 3 units of microprocessor, each tablet uses 4 units of circuit_board, and each smartphone combines 1 unit of microprocessor, 4 units of circuit_board, and 3 units of steel_rod. On the inbound side, microprocessor stock is managed with a min-max style replenishment rule, triggering orders when inventory falls below 3602.53 units and bringing it back up to 11700.53 units. Its carrying cost is $6.738 per unit, the shortage penalty is $3.637 per unit, and planners review that position every 15 weeks. Steel_rod is sourced on demand. Inside the plant, circuit_board starts with 4202809 units on hand and carries a holding cost of $1.931 per unit, reviewed every 1 week. Finished goods are also stocked: SoC begins at 450023 units with a holding cost of $0.752 per unit and a 1-week review cycle; tablet opens with 224268 units, costs $9.709 per unit to hold, incurs a shortage cost of $15.167 per unit, and is checked every 1 week; smartphone starts at 3087413 units, with holding cost of $8.98 per unit, shortage cost of $13.123 per unit, and the same 1-week review cadence. Supplier terms are quite different across vendors. Memory Star supplies microprocessor with a fixed 28.0 weeks lead time at $499.444 per unit, and invoices are settled between 38.0 weeks and 62.0 weeks after receipt. MetalFab Inc. is the alternate microprocessor source, much faster at exactly 9.0 weeks, priced at $194.298 per unit, with payment due 26.0 weeks after delivery and a maximum order size of 145713 units. For steel_rod, GlobalComponents delivers in 19.0 weeks at $33.881 per unit with payment terms ranging from 9.0 weeks to 44.0 weeks, while SiliconWorks ships in 17.0 weeks at $395.756 per unit and is paid 88.0 weeks after delivery. Production at the Fab uses 2 named processes. In the bonding step that makes SoC from steel_rod and microprocessor, cycle time varies uniformly between 5.0 weeks and 10.0 weeks, and the work requires the Conveyor Belt. That Conveyor Belt has capacity for 1 unit at a time, runs with a fixed 3.125 weeks service time, batches are enabled at 24 units, and jobs can wait up to 19.505 weeks before release; operating cost is $10.749 per week. Tablet production uses a welding step that converts circuit_board into finished tablets in exactly 23.0 weeks, with no specific shared resource listed. Smartphone production is another bonding activity, using microprocessor, circuit_board, and steel_rod, and it runs at a constant 29.0 weeks per cycle. An Injection Molder is also part of the resource pool with capacity of 5 units, a fixed 5.584 weeks service time, and an operating cost of $5.811 per week. Customer demand is handled product by product from the downstream side. Ross Associates buys SoC, placing orders every 50.0 weeks, with demand anywhere from 312171.0 units to 2814969.0 units per order. Delivery to that customer takes 22.0 weeks, unmet demand is backordered, the selling price is $2294.553 per unit, and customer payment arrives between 49.0 weeks and 53.0 weeks later. Lenovo Group purchases tablet on a more variable cadence, ordering between 26.0 weeks and 45.0 weeks apart, with order sizes ranging from 1344978.0 units to 4462152.0 units. Shipments reach Lenovo Group in 14.0 weeks to 24.0 weeks, available stock is shipped immediately and the remainder is backordered, the sale price is $3509.187 per unit, and payment comes exactly 74.0 weeks after delivery. NXP Semiconductors takes smartphone orders between 12.0 weeks and 25.0 weeks apart, each one fixed at 2970299.0 units, with customer lead time between 3.0 weeks and 13.0 weeks. Here the business follows a partial shipment policy: available units are delivered and any shortfall is lost. Smartphones sell at $4166.2 per unit and are paid for 28.0 weeks later. Structurally, all 4 suppliers feed the Fab, and the Fab sends all 3 finished products—SoC, tablet, and smartphone—to the Regional Depot. The scenario runs for a 100-week horizon with 1 replication.
