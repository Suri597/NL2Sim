This network is set up to build laptops through Main Factory, with Fulfillment Hub handling downstream storage and shipment. The raw inputs are epoxy_compound, gold_wire, glass_fiber, and silicon_wafer. Upstream, those materials are also turned into two pre-assembled parts before final build: sensor_array uses 4 units of epoxy_compound, 1 unit of silicon_wafer, 1 unit of gold_wire, and 2 units of glass_fiber, while lens_assembly is made from 1 unit of gold_wire and 4 units of glass_fiber. Each finished laptop then consumes 4 units of epoxy_compound, 3 units of gold_wire, 2 units of sensor_array, 3 units of glass_fiber, 1 unit of silicon_wafer, and 4 units of lens_assembly. Epoxy_compound is restocked on a periodic schedule in quantities ranging from 31278.0 units to 51625.0 units, with deliveries every 15.0 hours; it carries a holding cost of $4.079 per unit, a shortage cost of $28.412 per unit, and is reviewed every 1 hour. Gold_wire follows a min-max style rule, triggering replenishment when stock drops below 4139.63 units and bringing inventory up to 8453.53 units, with a shortage penalty of $21.794 per unit and a review every 29 hours. Glass_fiber also comes in periodic batches, anywhere from 13875.0 units to 89328.0 units, arriving every 18.0 hours; its holding cost is $5.8 per unit, shortage cost is $10.757 per unit, and review happens every 13 hours. Silicon_wafer is replenished in fixed lots of 8638.0 units, with arrival timing somewhere between 16.0 hours and 24.0 hours, and it has a holding cost of $0.546 per unit, a shortage cost of $15.431 per unit, and a 15-hour review cycle. On-hand starting stock is very large for the in-process items and finished goods: 2996967 units of sensor_array, 1306639 units of lens_assembly, and 4854911 units of laptop. Lens_assembly adds a holding cost of $5.504 per unit and is reviewed every 1 hour, while laptop inventory is reviewed every 1 hour and faces a shortage cost of $5.584 per unit. Supply relationships are pretty distinct. BatteryTech provides epoxy_compound with an exponential lead time averaging 1.0 hour, charges $433.253 per unit, allows payment between 13.0 hours and 79.0 hours after receipt, and can supply up to 237384 units per order; from BatteryTech to Main Factory, transfer takes 2.0 hours. CarbonSupply Co. handles gold_wire with an exponential lead time averaging 8.0 hours, a unit cost of $13.479, and fixed 60.0-hour payment terms. MetalFab Inc. supplies glass_fiber in exactly 9.0 hours, at $166.132 per unit, with payment due between 8.0 hours and 39.0 hours, and a delivery limit of 195894 units. MegaSupply covers silicon_wafer with a uniform lead time of 10.0 hours to 10.0 hours, effectively always 10.0 hours, priced at $227.46 per unit and paid 42.0 hours after delivery; shipping from MegaSupply to Main Factory takes 2.0 hours. Main Factory manages gold_wire, epoxy_compound, laptop, sensor_array, silicon_wafer, glass_fiber, and lens_assembly, and its molding step combines epoxy_compound, gold_wire, sensor_array, glass_fiber, silicon_wafer, and lens_assembly into laptop output. That molding cycle is fixed at 7.0 hours and requires the Welding Station. The Welding Station has capacity for 1 unit at a time, works with an exponential service time averaging 3.283 hours, incurs an operating cost of $10.221 per hour, stays up for a consistent 265.161 hours between failures, and when it goes down the repair time averages 11.001 hours. Finished laptops move from Main Factory to Fulfillment Hub. Demand comes from two major customers. ABB Industrial places laptop orders with exponentially distributed arrivals averaging 18.0 hours, and each order is roughly 3064910.0 units with variability of about 683755.0 units. ABB expects delivery in 23.0 hours, pays $3339.449 per unit, settles invoices 21.0 hours later, and under shortages receives available units immediately while the remainder is backordered. Its customer lead time is 27.0 hours, the selling price is $4249.158 per unit, payment arrives between 41.0 hours and 62.0 hours, and it follows the same partial-fulfillment-with-backorder approach.
