Its inventory is reviewed every 1 day, with a holding cost of $3.608 per unit and a shortage cost of $25.919 per unit. Carbon_fiber comes from Memory Star to the Fab as well, but this one is supplied in periodic batches ranging from 37820.0 units to 76355.0 units, arriving every 1 day. Supplier lead time is exponentially distributed with a mean of 25 days, the material costs $47.05 per unit, and payment terms run between 8 days and 38 days. Carbon_fiber stock is checked every 24 days, and shortages cost $24.484 per unit. Steel_rod is purchased from NanoMaterials Inc. and follows a min-max style replenishment rule: when inventory drops below 654.95 units, it is restored up to 930.11 units. Deliveries to the Fab take exactly 7 days, the purchase price is $436.083 per unit, and payment is made 26 days after delivery. Its inventory is reviewed every 6 days, with holding at $5.286 per unit and shortage at $4.681 per unit. Manufacturing happens at the Fab through a molding step that converts steel_rod, carbon_fiber, and glass_fiber into SoC, with each production cycle taking between 2 days and 7 days. On the finished goods side, SoC starts with 3987943 units in inventory and is reviewed every 1 day, while shortages are penalized at $28.86 per unit. Demand comes from ABB Industrial, which places an order every 42 days for exactly 2357358.0 units of SoC. Customer lead time falls between 5 days and 29 days, the selling price is $2151.215 per unit, and customer payment arrives 44 days later. If SoC is unavailable, the sale is lost rather than backordered. The network itself is straightforward: ChipCo, Memory Star, and NanoMaterials Inc. feed the Fab, and the Fab sends SoC to the Regional Depot. The model runs for 730 days, with the first 50 days treated as warm-up, and includes 10 replications.
