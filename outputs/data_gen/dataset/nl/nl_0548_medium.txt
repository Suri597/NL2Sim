This network centers on making smartphones, with CarbonSupply Co. and Process Go. feeding Plant A, and finished phones then moving on to the Logistics Center before serving customers like NXP Semiconductors and Samsung Electronics. Each smartphone uses 4 units of glass_fiber and 1 unit of solder_paste. Plant A handles solder_paste, glass_fiber, and smartphone inventory, and its bonding step converts those two inputs into smartphones in exactly 26.0 weeks per cycle. On the inbound side, glass_fiber is supplied by CarbonSupply Co. in periodic replenishments averaging 89152.0 weeks with variability of about 10058.0 weeks, with deliveries arriving somewhere between 16.0 weeks and 28.0 weeks; the supplier lead time itself averages 18.0 weeks. CarbonSupply Co. charges $402.227 per unit, allows payment between 33.0 weeks and 81.0 weeks after delivery, and can supply up to 988668 units per order. Glass_fiber carrying cost runs at $3.928 per unit, shortage cost is $7.568 per unit, and stock is reviewed every 10 weeks. Solder_paste comes from Process Go., with replenishment triggered when inventory drops below 1395.15 units and raised back to 4459.33 units. Its supplier lead time ranges from 8.0 weeks to 23.0 weeks, the purchase cost is $144.696 per unit, payment is due between 52.0 weeks and 87.0 weeks, and supply is capped at 813675 units per delivery. Holding solder_paste costs $3.739 per unit, shortages are expensive at $46.383 per unit, and review happens every 22 weeks. Finished smartphone stock starts at 3616378 units, with inventory checked every 1 week; holding cost is $1.695 per unit and shortage cost is $17.402 per unit. Demand comes from two major accounts. Samsung Electronics orders every 46.0 weeks, with demand ranging from 2733365.0 units to 3123522.0 units, expects delivery in exactly 11.0 weeks, pays $3976.711 per unit, and settles invoices 14.0 weeks later, with the same partial-fulfillment-and-lost-remainder approach when stock is tight. Structurally, the node set links CarbonSupply Co. and Process Go. with Plant A and the Logistics Center, and the material flow is straightforward: glass_fiber moves from CarbonSupply Co. to Plant A, solder_paste moves from Process Go. to Plant A, and smartphones move from Plant A to the Logistics Center. The scenario is run over a 200-week horizon, with results collected after a 30-week warm-up period and 10 replications.
