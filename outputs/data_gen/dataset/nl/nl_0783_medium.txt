This setup revolves around two finished goods, industrial_sensor and drone, built from memory_chip and carbon_fiber and moved through BatteryTech, MetalFab Inc., the Assembly Hub, and a Warehouse. The industrial_sensor uses 2 units of carbon_fiber and 4 units of memory_chip per unit, while the drone requires 3 units of memory_chip per unit. Both raw materials are sourced on an as-needed basis rather than on a fixed replenishment schedule. For memory_chip, inventory is reviewed every 2 hours, with a holding cost of $5.116 per unit and a shortage cost of $30.092 per unit. BatteryTech supplies that item at $364.511 per unit, with a consistent 8.0-hour lead time, and payment is made 47.0 days after delivery. Carbon_fiber is also purchased in response to demand, but its stock position is checked every 10 hours; carrying it costs $9.967 per unit and shortages run $18.781 per unit. MetalFab Inc. provides carbon_fiber at $246.592 per unit, with delivery varying between 1.0 and 12.0 hours, payment terms ranging from 28.0 to 50.0 days, and a maximum order size of 6259 units. Manufacturing happens at the Assembly Hub through a molding step. One molding flow converts carbon_fiber and memory_chip into industrial_sensor in exactly 12.0 hours, and that facility manages industrial_sensor, memory_chip, and carbon_fiber inventory together. A second molding flow at the same Assembly Hub turns memory_chip into drone, with processing time ranging from 8.0 to 20.0 hours, and this side manages drone and memory_chip stock. Finished items then move from the Assembly Hub to the Warehouse, with both industrial_sensor and drone stored there for fulfillment. On the product side, industrial_sensor starts with 1284725 units in inventory, is reviewed every 1 hour, and carries a shortage penalty of $19.997 per unit. Demand comes from TechCorp, with customer orders arriving at an average interval of 52.0 hours, each order fixed at 4649718.0 units. Delivery to TechCorp takes exactly 4.0 hours, the product sells at $3309.728 per unit, customer payment comes between 8.0 and 62.0 days later, and if stock is unavailable the sale is simply lost. Drone begins with 4493429 units on hand, is also reviewed every 1 hour, has a holding cost of $5.666 per unit, and a shortage cost of $19.134 per unit. Dell Technologies places drone orders with an average arrival interval of 26.0 hours, each for 1550496.0 units, and receives shipments in between 16.0 and 26.0 hours. The drone sells at $1665.825 per unit, payment is collected 36.0 days after delivery, and shortages are handled as lost sales here as well. The network is straightforward: BatteryTech and MetalFab Inc. feed the Assembly Hub with raw materials, and the Assembly Hub sends both industrial_sensor and drone to the Warehouse. The model runs for 500 hours, with the first 50 hours treated as warm-up, and uses 1 replication.
