Demand comes from Lenovo Group, with orders arriving uniformly between 17 weeks and 26 weeks, and each order is a steady 3597253 units. The network itself is straightforward: ChipCo supplies ceramic_substrate to Advanced Fab, and Advanced Fab sends EV_battery output to the Fulfillment Hub.
