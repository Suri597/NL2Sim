This setup centers on producing FPGA units, with Process Go. and PrecisionParts feeding the operation and the Fab turning inputs into finished goods before they move on to the Logistics Center. Each FPGA requires 4 units of solder_paste and 4 units of memory_chip. At the raw material level, solder_paste is managed with a min-max style replenishment rule: when stock drops below 1435.96 units, it is brought back up to 4371.98 units. That item carries a holding cost of $7.884 per unit, a shortage cost of $7.364 per unit, and it is reviewed every 16 weeks. Process Go. supplies the solder_paste at $246.966 per unit, with lead times averaging 27.0 weeks, payment terms ranging uniformly between 10.0 weeks and 30.0 weeks, and a maximum delivery size of 185764 units. Memory_chip is handled similarly but with different thresholds, reordering once inventory falls under 2234.7 units and topping back up to 4428.05 units. Its shortage cost is $11.78 per unit, and the stock position is checked every 19 weeks. PrecisionParts sells the memory_chip for $184.256 per unit, typically delivers with an average lead time of 8.0 weeks, offers a fixed 19.0-week payment term, and can supply up to 929676 units per order. Manufacturing happens at the Fab through a coating operation that consumes solder_paste and memory_chip and outputs FPGA units, with each production cycle taking between 16.0 weeks and 21.0 weeks. The Fab manages all three inventories—solder_paste, memory_chip, and FPGA—and completed FPGA units are then sent to the Logistics Center. On the finished goods side, FPGA starts with 1426038 units in inventory, is reviewed every 1 week, and has a shortage cost of $29.619 per unit. Demand comes from Schneider Electric, with order arrivals occurring somewhere between 15.0 weeks and 56.0 weeks, and each order size ranging from 2664530.0 units to 3265632.0 units. Customer lead time runs between 3.0 weeks and 17.0 weeks. If demand exceeds available stock, available FPGA units ship right away and the rest is backordered. The selling price is $2138.184 per unit, and Schneider Electric pays exactly 29.0 days after delivery. The network itself is straightforward: Process Go. sends solder_paste to the Fab, PrecisionParts sends memory_chip to the Fab, and the Fab forwards FPGA output to the Logistics Center. The model is run over 365 weeks, with results collected after a 30-week warm-up period.
