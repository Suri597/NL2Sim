This network makes routers through a setup that links ChipCo, BatteryTech, GlassCore Ltd., and ElectroParts with the Fab and a downstream Distribution Center. The finished router then brings together 4 units of plastic_pellet, 1 unit of power_module, 4 units of steel_rod, 1 unit of silicon_wafer, 1 unit of display_panel, 3 units of battery_pack, and 3 units of epoxy_compound.
