This network revolves around four purchased inputs—gold_wire, memory_chip, aluminum_sheet, and carbon_fiber—which are turned into a fabricated sub-component called frame_structure and then into two sellable items, medical_device and EV_battery. The frame_structure itself consumes 4 units of memory_chip, 3 units of aluminum_sheet, 4 units of gold_wire, and 1 unit of carbon_fiber per unit produced. Building one medical_device then requires 4 units of aluminum_sheet, 3 units of gold_wire, 3 units of carbon_fiber, and 3 units of frame_structure, while each EV_battery uses 2 units of frame_structure, 1 unit of memory_chip, and 2 units of carbon_fiber. On the inbound side, gold_wire is restocked on a very long fixed cycle of 40647.0 weeks, with replenishment arrivals falling between 16.0 weeks and 22.0 weeks; it carries a holding charge of $5.37 per unit, a shortage penalty of $15.632 per unit, and is reviewed every 30 weeks. memory_chip is sourced on demand, with a shortage cost of $23.09 per unit and a 30-week review cadence. aluminum_sheet follows a min-max style rule, triggering around 3877.93 units and replenishing up to 10411.13 units, with holding cost of $5.851 per unit and review every 6 weeks. carbon_fiber is also managed by stock position, with a reorder point of 2453.57 units and an order-up-to level of 4411.39 units. There are 236533 units of frame_structure on hand at the start, along with 230035 units of medical_device and 1439960 units of EV_battery; the medical_device stock incurs a shortage cost of $46.109 per unit and is checked every 1 week, while EV_battery inventory has a holding cost of $9.077 per unit and is also reviewed every 1 week. Suppliers are split across the materials: GlassCore Ltd. provides gold_wire with an exponential lead time averaging 15.0 weeks, at $284.888 per unit, on net 85.0 days payment terms, and can supply up to 427908 units per order; MegaSupply is a second gold_wire source with the same 15.0-week average lead time, a higher price of $346.722 per unit, 51.0 days to pay, and capacity of 128251 units. CarbonSupply Co. supplies memory_chip with an exponential lead time averaging 24.0 weeks, a cost of $306.93 per unit, payment due between 50.0 days and 74.0 days, and a maximum order size of 952235 units, while SiliconWorks offers the same material in exactly 13.0 weeks at $484.946 per unit with 88.0-day payment. For aluminum_sheet, Process Go. ships in 12.0 to 24.0 weeks at $70.686 per unit with payment 39.0 days after receipt, and PrecisionParts offers a much faster fixed 2.0-week lead time at $433.379 per unit with 85.0-day terms. The physical network links all 7 suppliers to 3 internal sites—Plant A, Main Factory, and the Distribution Center. Some lanes have extra transit time on top of supplier lead time: CarbonSupply Co. to Plant A takes another 7.0 to 9.0 weeks, Process Go. to Plant A adds 2.0 to 6.0 weeks, and BatteryTech to Plant A adds 7.0 to 13.0 weeks. Main Factory manages frame_structure, memory_chip, EV_battery, and carbon_fiber, and performs bonding to turn frame_structure, memory_chip, and carbon_fiber into EV_battery in a steady 23.0-week cycle. The network also includes a Conveyor Belt resource with capacity of 3 units, processing time between 4.376 weeks and 9.777 weeks, uptime averaging 209.981 weeks, downtime of 2.742 weeks, and operating cost of $1.755 per week. Finished goods flow from Plant A to the Distribution Center for medical_device and from Main Factory to the Distribution Center for EV_battery. Demand comes from two customers: Siemens Digital buys medical_device, placing orders every 18.0 to 36.0 weeks, with demand centered around 1181589.0 units and varying by about 224259.0 units; deliveries to Siemens Digital take exactly 2.0 weeks, available stock ships immediately and the remainder is backordered, the selling price is $723.242 per unit, and payment is received in 38.0 to 57.0 days.
