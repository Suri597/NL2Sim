This network makes two end products, smartphone and GPU, through a shared Processing Unit and a Fulfillment Hub over a 200-day planning window. The raw inputs are gold_wire, carbon_fiber, ceramic_substrate, and copper_wire, and there is also an in-house built sub-assembly called battery_pack. Each battery_pack consumes 2 units of copper_wire, 3 units of ceramic_substrate, 4 units of carbon_fiber, and 2 units of gold_wire. Smartphone assembly uses 2 units of ceramic_substrate, 3 units of gold_wire, and 4 units of copper_wire per unit, while each GPU requires 3 units of carbon_fiber plus 2 units of battery_pack. Gold_wire is sourced on an as-needed basis, with inventory reviewed every 18 days, a holding cost of $0.741 per unit, and a shortage cost of $27.11 per unit. It comes either from GlobalComponents, which delivers in between 17 and 21 days at $431.208 per unit with payment due between 9 and 73 days after receipt, or from PolymerSource, which has a fixed 3-day lead time, charges $498.694 per unit, offers between 26 and 80 days to pay, and can supply up to 902381 units per order. Carbon_fiber is also demand-triggered, reviewed every 17 days, with a holding cost of $1.141 per unit and a shortage penalty of $35.071 per unit. Supply can come from GlassCore Ltd. in anywhere from 1 to 24 days at $72.629 per unit with net 24 days payment, or from PrecisionParts with an exponential lead time averaging 26 days, a price of $343.179 per unit, 15-day payment terms, a maximum order size of 381709 units, and transfer time ranging from 5 to 11 days to the Processing Unit. Ceramic_substrate follows the same demand-led replenishment logic, is reviewed every 10 days, carries $9.329 per unit in holding cost and $9.515 per unit in shortage cost, and is purchased either from BatteryTech, with an exponential lead time averaging 8 days, a cost of $345.762 per unit, payment between 7 and 39 days, and capacity of 793721 units, or from Memory Star, with an exponential lead time averaging 12 days, a cost of $330.693 per unit, and payment due between 14 and 49 days. Copper_wire works differently: it is restocked on a regular schedule in lots averaging 40762.0 units, varying by about 2821.0 units, with deliveries arriving somewhere between 8.0 and 20.0 days. Its inventory is reviewed every 19 days and held at $6.182 per unit. MegaSupply provides it with supplier lead times between 1.0 and 25.0 days, a unit cost of $306.07, payment between 11.0 and 37.0 days, and a fixed 12.0-day transfer to the Processing Unit. On the production side, the Processing Unit handles smartphone assembly using ceramic_substrate, gold_wire, and copper_wire, with each run taking between 2.0 and 21.0 days and requiring the Conveyor Belt. That Conveyor Belt has capacity for 3 units, a constant service time of 4.931 days, and batching is turned on at 44 units with a maximum wait of 16.228 days. It also breaks down in a very real-world way: uptime is a steady 347.236 days, downtime averages 44.291 days, and operating expense runs at $1.916 per day. The same Processing Unit also performs GPU bonding from carbon_fiber and battery_pack, and that cycle ranges from 17.0 to 21.0 days. No named resource is required for that step, although a CNC Machine is available in the system with capacity of 3 units, a constant service time of 5.848 days, and an operating cost of $19.257 per day. Inventory on hand starts at 131183 units for battery_pack, 1198391 units for smartphone, and 2568079 units for GPU. Battery_pack inventory is reviewed every 1 day and costs $9.483 per unit to hold, while GPU stock is also reviewed every 1 day with a very light holding charge of $0.01 per unit. The Processing Unit manages smartphone, gold_wire, ceramic_substrate, and copper_wire in one flow, and battery_pack, GPU, and carbon_fiber in another, with both smartphone and GPU then moving onward to the Fulfillment Hub. On the demand side, NXP Semiconductors buys smartphone with orders arriving between 8.0 and 36.0 days, each order fixed at 247865.0 units. Delivery lead time to that customer is exactly 8.0 days, the selling price is $3592.387 per unit, and payment is received 19.0 days after delivery. If stock is short, available units are shipped and the remainder is lost. ABB Industrial buys GPU on a stricter rhythm, with orders every 7.0 days for 4079128.0 units, a customer lead time of 29.0 days, a selling price of $4793.961 per unit, and cash collected 75.0 days later. For GPU demand, unmet demand is lost entirely. The node structure ties GlobalComponents, GlassCore Ltd., BatteryTech, MegaSupply, PolymerSource, PrecisionParts, and Memory Star into the Processing Unit and Fulfillment Hub, creating a fairly supplier-heavy operation with both premium electronics content and high-volume outbound fulfillment, all modeled in 1 replication across 200 days.
