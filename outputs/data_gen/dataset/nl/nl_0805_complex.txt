This network centers on Advanced Fab, which makes laptops and also keeps the key inputs on hand, with Regional Depot serving as the downstream warehouse. The material base includes glass_fiber, solder_paste, and ceramic_substrate, and there is also a fabricated sub-component called cooling_unit. Each cooling_unit uses 3 units of glass_fiber, 2 units of ceramic_substrate, and 1 unit of solder_paste. Each laptop then requires 4 units of ceramic_substrate, 4 units of cooling_unit, 1 unit of glass_fiber, and 4 units of solder_paste.
