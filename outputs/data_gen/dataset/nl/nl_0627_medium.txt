This setup centers on producing FPGA units through a network that links MetalFab Inc., FastChip Corp., and GlobalComponents with the Integration Center and a downstream Regional Depot. The Integration Center manages memory_chip, plastic_pellet, silicon_wafer, and FPGA inventory, while the completed FPGA units then move on to the Regional Depot.
