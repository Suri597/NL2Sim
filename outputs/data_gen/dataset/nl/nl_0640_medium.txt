This setup revolves around making smartphones through a network that links ElectroParts, GlassCore Ltd., and PolymerSource to the Pilot Line, with finished phones then moving on to the Distribution Center. The bill of materials is layered: each sensor_array is built from 4 units of ceramic_substrate and 1 unit of resin, and each smartphone then requires 2 units of resin, 2 units of ceramic_substrate, and 2 units of sensor_array. At the raw material level, ceramic_substrate is restocked on a very long fixed cycle of 29989.0 days, with deliveries arriving periodically somewhere between 4.0 days and 8.0 days, and its shortage penalty is $15.252 per unit with inventory reviewed every 15 days. Resin is sourced on demand, carries a holding cost of $4.564 per unit and a shortage cost of $7.269 per unit, and is reviewed every 20 days. On the supply side, ElectroParts ships ceramic_substrate to the Pilot Line with a consistent 3.0-day lead time, charges $87.685 per unit, offers payment terms between 17.0 days and 22.0 days after delivery, and can supply up to 666462 units per order. PolymerSource also feeds ceramic_substrate into the Pilot Line, but with an exponential lead time averaging 25.0 days, at $89.365 per unit, and payment due between 29.0 days and 74.0 days. Resin comes from GlassCore Ltd., which delivers in anywhere from 2.0 days to 13.0 days, charges $53.795 per unit, and is paid exactly 67.0 days after receipt. Inside the operation, the Pilot Line manages resin, sensor_array, ceramic_substrate, and smartphone inventory, and runs a polishing step that consumes resin, ceramic_substrate, and sensor_array to produce smartphones in a fixed 6.0-day cycle. There is already a very large stock position in place, with 1067256 units of sensor_array on hand and 4177790 units of smartphone inventory ready at the start; sensor_array holding cost is $5.487 per unit and that stock is reviewed every 1 day. Customer demand comes from two major buyers. Samsung Electronics places smartphone orders at intervals between 25.0 days and 38.0 days, with demand averaging 86575.0 units and varying by about 6965.0 units, expects delivery in exactly 7.0 days, pays $1482.428 per unit, and settles invoices between 46.0 days and 83.0 days after delivery; if stock is unavailable, the sale is lost. Broadcom Limited orders smartphones every 54.0 days flat, with demand centered around 4535161.0 units and variability of 487099.0 units, receives shipments in 29.0 days, buys at $2903.456 per unit, and pays exactly 52.0 days later, again under a lost-sales arrangement. The modeled network therefore runs supplier-to-Pilot Line flows for ceramic_substrate and resin, then sends finished smartphones from the Pilot Line to the Distribution Center, and the scenario is evaluated over a 200-day horizon with 5 replications.
