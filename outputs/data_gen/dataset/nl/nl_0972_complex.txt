This network revolves around a Production Center feeding a Logistics Center with three finished goods: FPGA, laptop, and drone. The plant buys in ceramic_substrate, epoxy_compound, plastic_pellet, and aluminum_sheet, and it also builds a fabricated sub-component called cooling_unit before using it in drone production. Each cooling_unit consumes 5 units of plastic_pellet, 2 units of ceramic_substrate, 2 units of aluminum_sheet, and 1 unit of epoxy_compound. The FPGA itself is made from 4 units of aluminum_sheet and 1 unit of ceramic_substrate, the laptop uses 2 units of epoxy_compound and 2 units of plastic_pellet, and each drone requires 2 units of cooling_unit, 4 units of plastic_pellet, and 2 units of aluminum_sheet.
