This setup centers on making CPU units through a network that links ElectroParts, GlassCore Ltd., SiliconWorks, the Manufacturing Site, and a Storage Facility, with ABB Industrial as the customer. The raw inputs are gold_wire and steel_rod, and there is also an in-house built sub-assembly called battery_pack that uses 3 units of gold_wire and 1 unit of steel_rod for each pack. The finished CPU requires 2 units of steel_rod, 4 units of gold_wire, and 4 units of battery_pack per unit, so the Manufacturing Site is handling both component consumption and final assembly before CPUs move on to the Storage Facility. Gold_wire inventory is managed with a min-max approach: when stock drops below 3364.3 units, it is replenished up to 9235.63 units. Its carrying cost is $0.889 per unit, shortage cost is $27.771 per unit, and inventory is reviewed every 25 hours. Steel_rod is sourced on demand, with a holding cost of $9.5 per unit, a shortage penalty of $39.802 per unit, and a review cadence of 23 hours. The battery_pack stock starts at 3029007 units, carries a holding cost of $3.616 per unit, has a shortage cost of $45.584 per unit, and is reviewed every 1 hour. Finished CPU inventory begins at 2190072 units. On the supply side, ElectroParts provides gold_wire with an exponential lead time averaging 9.0 hours, at a purchase cost of $248.975 per unit, and payment is due 71.0 days after delivery. SiliconWorks is the second source for gold_wire, with an exponential lead time averaging 26.0 hours, a lower cost of $202.872 per unit, and 29.0-day payment terms. Steel_rod comes from GlassCore Ltd. with a fixed 27.0-hour lead time, a supplier price of $241.367 per unit, and payment due 79.0 days after receipt. The main production resource is a Robot Arm with capacity for 5 units, and its processing time ranges uniformly from 5.65 hours to 5.972 hours while costing $12.728 per hour to operate. Even so, the assembly step at the Manufacturing Site itself runs on a consistent 21.0-hour cycle to convert steel_rod, gold_wire, and battery_pack into CPU units. Customer demand comes from ABB Industrial, with order arrivals following an exponential pattern averaging 16.0 hours, and each order is for exactly 4457740.0 units of CPU. Delivery to the customer takes 17.0 hours, and the sales price is $3696.626 per unit. If inventory is short, available units are shipped immediately and the remaining demand is backordered. ABB Industrial pays 89.0 days after delivery. The model runs for a horizon of 500 hours with 1 replication.
